# network

Your professional network across accounts, teams, and industry peers. Not a cold CRM table, but living context: what each stakeholder cares about, how decisions get made around them, what commitments exist, and how relationships evolve across missions.

## Key Circles

Organize contacts and stakeholder dynamics by circle (client sponsors, delivery teams, executive peers, external partners/experts). Link people observations both here and to the relevant engagement or responsibility context map.

## Tensions

Note misaligned incentives between stakeholders, sponsor turnover, or sensitive communication dynamics that affect upcoming meetings or deliverables.

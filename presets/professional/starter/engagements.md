# engagements

Bounded missions and projects. Each engagement context map captures the client/account, your explicit role or expertise on that mission (for example: Lead Data Architect, Advisory Consultant, Delivery Lead), active parallel workstreams, and associated deliverables.

## Active Engagements

Add one context map per major engagement as soon as it accumulates 5+ insights or multiple parallel workstreams. Keep smaller missions as sections here until they grow.

Example structure for a complex project (such as a Data Platform build):
- Mission map: `[[acme-data-platform]]` (states client, your role, overall framing, and deliverable links)
- Parallel workstream maps (split out once a stream reaches 5+ insights): `[[acme-data-platform-ingestion]]`, `[[acme-data-platform-governance]]`

## Tensions & Decisions Needed

Track cross-engagement resource conflicts, scope drift, or open arbitrations that affect delivery commitments.

## Explorations Needed

- For each active engagement, is your role/posture clearly stated in the map frontmatter?
- Are any workstreams stagnating silently based on their `updated` dates?
- Which insights from current missions deserve cross-linking to [[expertise]]?
